SUMMARY

Kneecap is a vibrant musical dramedy that explores the intersection of hip-hop culture and Irish identity in Belfast. Following the journey of a teacher and two aspiring rappers, the film highlights themes of friendship and language preservation through a humorous and uplifting lens. With a unique blend of true story elements and semi-fiction, it celebrates the power of music to unite and inspire.
