SUMMARY

Seven Samurai is a landmark film that explores themes of class relations and collectivism through the story of a group of samurai who protect a village from bandits. Directed by Akira Kurosawa, it combines action and drama with a reflective tone, showcasing the moral ambiguities faced by its characters. The film's black-and-white cinematography and epic battle sequences contribute to its status as a classic in world cinema.
