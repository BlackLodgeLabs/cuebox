Each past night is a cinema ticket. Scroll to load more, or tap a ticket to open that night’s programme, dated in the title. To remove a night, swipe the ticket left to reveal Remove, or long-press it — both open a confirm box first. A tap only shrinks the ticket slightly; there is no amber on press.
